Section 10 • Governance & Privacy

Privacy & Responsible-Feedback Notice

Anonymous vs. Identified Mode: Students may submit feedback anonymously without entering a name, university email, phone number, or student ID. If you opt for Identified Mode to receive follow-up updates, your personal details are visible strictly to authorised VGU Mess Administrators and are never displayed publicly.

Technical Transparency: While anonymous submissions omit personal identity fields, standard network/security logs are used for rate-limiting and abuse prevention; therefore, the portal does not make exaggerated claims of cryptographic anonymity.

Constructive Use: Keep comments focused on food quality, hygiene, portion size, and dining operations. Personal defamation, threats, or spam are prohibited under university policy.

Section 15 • Institutional Governance

Privacy Notice, Terms of Use & Feedback Moderation Policy

1. What Data Is Collected & Why

We collect meal ratings, food quality tags, written feedback, and optional food photographs solely to monitor and improve hostel mess services at Vivekananda Global University (VGU), Jaipur. In Anonymous Mode, no name, email address, phone number, or student ID is required. In Identified Mode, optional contact details are collected solely so authorised wardens or mess committee members can follow up regarding your report.

2. Technical Metadata & Honest Anonymity Disclosure

To protect the portal against automated spam, denial-of-service attacks, and duplicate submissions, the server processes standard request metadata (such as rate-limiting hashes and submission timestamps in IST). Accordingly, while Anonymous Mode strips personal identity fields from your submission record, the university does not promise absolute cryptographic anonymity against lawful security audits.

3. Access Controls & Data Retention

Individual complaints, student names, and uploaded evidence photos are never displayed publicly. Only authorised VGU administrators with role-based permissions can access raw records. Personal identifiers on resolved tickets are retained for 180 days before automated anonymization, while aggregate statistical ratings are retained for long-term quality benchmarking.

4. Responsible Feedback & Moderation Policy

Submissions must focus on food quality, hygiene, portion size, menu variety, and dining room operations. Abusive language, personal harassment of mess staff, fabricated photographs, or spam are strictly prohibited. Student-submitted concerns are treated as unverified reports until inspected and confirmed by the VGU Mess Committee.
